**Purpose** — Explain how Cloud plans differ, so you can pick one without hunting through the pricing page. Prices and exact limits change; the [Pricing](https://notploy.com/pricing) page is the source of truth.

<Callout type="info">
Plan names, prices and per-plan limits are updated over time. Always confirm the current numbers
on [notploy.com/pricing](https://notploy.com/pricing) before committing to a plan.
</Callout>

## How plans differ

Cloud plans are primarily distinguished by:

- **How many servers** you can connect (and the price per additional server).
- **Team size and organizations** (users, organizations, environments).
- **Backups and scheduled jobs** (how many per service).
- **RBAC** (basic roles vs. fine-grained custom roles).
- **Security and governance** (2FA, SSO/SAML, SCIM, audit logs).
- **Support level** (community, email & chat, priority with SLA).

The **deployment engine is identical** on every plan — plans govern capacity, teams and governance, not how applications are built or run.

## Typical plan shapes

| Plan | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Hobby** | Solo developers on a single server. |
| **Startup** | Small teams needing multiple environments, backups and scheduled jobs. |
| **Enterprise** | SSO/SAML, SCIM, audit logs, custom roles and priority support. |
| **Agency** | Managing infrastructure for multiple clients. |

Exact inclusion per plan is listed on the pricing page; the [feature matrix](/docs/core/reference/feature-matrix) compares editions and Enterprise capabilities.

<Callout type="info">
Annual billing is typically cheaper than monthly. See the pricing page for current discounts.
</Callout>

## What is the same, regardless of plan

- Same deployment engine and API.
- Same [project / environment / service](/docs/core/concepts) model.
- Same domain and TLS handling.
- Same migration and backup behavior.
